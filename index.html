About Me

Brahim Walid is a PhD candidate at Hosei University, Japan, specializing in context-aware vital signs monitoring using FMCW radar under Prof. Jianhua Ma. He is driven by an interest in the intersection of hardware and software to develop practical wireless sensing solutions for healthcare applications.

My research focuses on improving the reliability of vital sign measurements through context-awareness and advanced signal processing techniques. I'm passionate about creating technology that can make a meaningful impact on healthcare monitoring and patient well-being.

My Research

Context-Aware Elaborate Vital Signs Monitoring using FMCW Radar

My PhD research focuses on improving vital sign measurement reliability using context-awareness (posture, motion, etc.) and FMCW radar within the WiSPaC framework. This work aims to enhance healthcare monitoring through non-contact sensing technologies.
