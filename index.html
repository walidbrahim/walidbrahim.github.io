About

PhD candidate at Hosei University (Prof. Jianhua Ma, Ubiquitous Computing Lab) working on contactless health monitoring with FMCW mmWave radar, after four years as an R&D engineer in electronics manufacturing. Research interests: FMCW radar signal processing, physiological sensing, multi-sensor fusion, machine learning for time series, sensing robots, and LLM agents for research tooling. 18 IEEE/ACM papers, 6 as first author. Based in Tokyo.

Projects

Wi²Sense

Radar sensing platform: room presence from point clouds, indoor localisation, respiration and heartbeat, sleep and activity. Wi²Sleep is its sleep-scenario application, with focus on trusted output, reliability and practicality.

Wi²Robot

A ROS 2 / Nav2 sensing robot that picks a viewpoint from a scene model, drives there, settles, and triggers the radar measurement. Now exploring radar-based semantic mapping of the room (Wi²Scene).

MeridianOS

Self-hosted research hub over a fleet of devices: local LLM inference gateway, agent policies, tool connectors, and customised coding agents with my own policies, hooks and MCP servers.
